I am currently a research assistant at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, working with Prof. Tong Zhang. Previously, I was a visiting student at Rutgers University (2024-2025), advised by Prof. Hao Wang. I obtained my B.S. in Electronic Information Engineering from Wuhan University in 2024.

My research focuses on multimodal models and enhancing their reasoning capabilities, with an emphasis on improving interpretability and leveraging formal languages like Lean to strengthen reasoning processes. My vision is to develop multimodal models that deeply understand the physical world.

Research Interests
======
**Multimodal Learning**: Developing frameworks to bridge visual and textual understanding, particularly in general reasoning and cross-modal alignment.

**Reinforcement Learning**: Utilizing reinforcement learning to enhance multimodal models' understanding and generalization abilities across diverse tasks.

**Trustworthy AI**: Investigating the interpretability of MLLMs to understand their underlying mechanisms, improve performance, and reduce hallucinations and modality gaps.

**Formal Languages**: Inspired by AlphaGeometry and Lean, I am increasingly interested in the potential of formal languages to augment reasoning capabilities in AI systems.
