## Welcome!

I am currently a **research intern** at NTU, advised by Prof. Yang Liu. Previously, I was a visiting student at Rutgers University (2024-2025), advised by Prof. Hao Wang. I obtained my **B.S. in Electronic Information Engineering** from Wuhan University in 2024.

---

## Research Vision

My research focuses on **multimodal models** and **enhancing their reasoning capabilities**, with emphasis on:
- **Interpretability** and explainability of AI systems
- Leveraging **formal languages** (like Lean) to strengthen reasoning
- Developing multimodal models that deeply understand the **physical world**

---

## Research Interests

| Area | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **🖼️ Multimodal Learning** | Developing frameworks to bridge visual and textual understanding, particularly in general reasoning and cross-modal alignment. |
| **🎲 Reinforcement Learning** | Utilizing RL to enhance multimodal models' understanding and generalization abilities across diverse tasks. |
| **Embodied AI** | Developing AI systems that connect perception, action, and reasoning for grounded interaction with the physical world. |
| **📐 Formal Languages** | Exploring the potential of formal languages to augment reasoning capabilities in AI systems (inspired by AlphaGeometry and Lean). |
